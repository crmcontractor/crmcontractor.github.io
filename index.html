Meet Deborah · Where It All Started

A System Built at My Kitchen Table

I’m Deborah Crutcher, a homeschool mom who simply wanted to make life a little easier. I’m also the founder of The CRM Contractor. The first system I built was for my own family.

I wanted homeschool to feel efficient for me and, as I like to say, easy peasy for my kids. I needed one place to keep up with assignments, attendance, grades, and report cards. My kids needed to know what to work on each day and how to ask for help when they got stuck. So I built our Homeschool Command Center.

I gave myself a parent dashboard and gave each child a hub of their own. I added daily assignment emails, automatic report cards, and a help request form. My kids could see what was due, and I could check their progress in one place.

When I returned to the workforce, I thought about how much time I could have saved in my own business if I’d had the right systems in place from the start. Separate tools and monthly subscriptions added up, and I still had to connect the dots myself.

That’s why I started The CRM Contractor. Today I build custom dashboards, client trackers, forms, and automations around the way you already work. We start with what you’re juggling, what you wish you could see at a glance, and what you’re tired of doing by hand. Then I build a system that fits your business, with a one-time setup price.

I never stopped learning. I earned my Excel Expert and Google certifications, I am working toward my CAPM, and I use AI tools responsibly to build faster. Every system is still planned, tested and checked by me, so you get something that really works for your life.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of systems do you build?

Anything you keep track of by hand or across too many places: client records, schedules, attendance, report cards, budgets, employee information, training, rent and maintenance, payments, reminders and dashboards. If you track it, I can organize it.

Do I need to be good with spreadsheets or technology?

Not at all. I build it so you and your team only click buttons and fill in simple forms. You also get instructions and a walkthrough when your system is delivered.

Will I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. Your system runs in your own free Google account, and once it is paid in full it is yours. There are no subscriptions or per-user charges.

How much does a custom system cost?

Every system is priced by its scope. After your free consultation you receive a written proposal with the exact price, what is included, what is not, and the turnaround time, before you commit to anything.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the size of the system. Your estimated turnaround is in your proposal, and your start date and estimated completion date are set as soon as your deposit is received.

How does payment work?

A deposit is due when you sign your agreement, and the remaining balance is due when you approve the finished system. I accept Cash App and Zelle.

Can I see my system while it is being built?

Yes. You get viewer access, dated progress updates and a private client portal where you approve each stage or ask for changes. Viewing does not transfer ownership. Your system is transferred to you after final payment.

What if I want changes?

Changes inside your agreed scope are included. If you ask for something outside the scope, you will see the added cost and any change to the timeline first, and nothing happens until you approve it.

Can you improve a system I already have?

Yes. Bring what you are using now, whether that is paper, spreadsheets or an app, and we will talk about whether to improve it or start fresh.

Is my information kept private?

Your system lives in your own Google account. Work samples are only shown with names and private details removed, and client reviews never show an email address.
